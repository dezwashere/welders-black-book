Welders Black Book Support

Welders Black Book is a welding reference app for students, apprentices, fabricators, and professional welders.

Common questions

Does the app require an account?
No.

Does the app work offline?
Core reference content, saved references, projects, and notes work offline. Internet access is required to create a shareable link for a saved reference.

Where is my saved information stored?
Saved references, projects, and notes are stored locally on your device.
